# 🌍 Arabic Morphology (الصرف في اللغة العربية)

## 📌 Overview

Arabic has **rich and complex morphology** compared to English.

---

## 💡 Key Features

- Prefixes: (و، ف، ب...)
- Suffixes: (ها، هم...)
- Root-based system (جذور)
- Gender, number, tense
- Dual form

---

## 🔥 Example
فسيأكلونها


### Meaning:

"And they will eat it


---

## 📊 Morphological Breakdown

| Part | Meaning |
|------|--------|
| ف | and |
| س | future |
| يأكل | eat |
| ون | plural |
| ها | it |

👉 One word = full sentence

---

## 🚨 Challenges

- Complex structure
- High ambiguity
- Many word forms
- Rich affix system

---

## 🚀 Key Insight

Arabic morphology requires advanced models due to its **rich structure and ambiguity**, making it more challenging than English in NLP.
